# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective customers, importers, distributors, and hospitality partners evaluating the Karlova Krčma beer range on desktop and mobile connections.

## Product Purpose

Present the Karlova Krčma range as a premium Czech brewing brand, communicate the character of each beer through product imagery and motion, and turn qualified interest into partner enquiries.

## Brand Personality

Craft-led, atmospheric, and confident. The experience should feel cinematic and premium while remaining direct and credible.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic beer templates, low-quality or visibly compressed product media, decorative motion that obscures the cans, and loading behavior that leaves empty cards or blocks access to content.

## Design Principles

- Let real product media carry the brand.
- Preserve image and video fidelity.
- Make the first useful frame immediate, even on a cold cache.
- Load motion progressively without competing for bandwidth.
- Keep interactions reliable across pointer, touch, and reduced-motion contexts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard access, readable contrast, meaningful media labels, and reduced-motion behavior. The core product information must remain available when autoplay is blocked or motion is disabled.
